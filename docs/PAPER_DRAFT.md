## Title

**Weakly-supervised localisation generalises where genotype prediction does not:
an externally-validated null for EGFR radiogenomics from lung CT**

Alternatives, less good but worth having on the page:
- *Attention supervised against tumour masks transfers across institutions; the
  EGFR prediction it was built to explain does not*
- *A ceiling for lesion-anchored EGFR radiogenomics, and what analytic choice is
  worth in this literature*

## Abstract (structured, ~250 words)

**Background.** CT-based prediction of EGFR mutation status is widely reported at
AUC 0.80–0.89, but almost always from single-centre cohorts of 200–800 patients
without external validation.

**Purpose.** To evaluate whether a grounded vision–language model predicts EGFR
status from lung CT beyond routinely available clinical variables, and whether its
supervised attention localises tumours on an unseen cohort.

**Materials and Methods.** Retrospective secondary analysis of NSCLC-Radiogenomics
(211 subjects; 153 with a known EGFR call, 40 mutant, 26.1%). A BiomedCLIP ViT-B/16
encoder with a region-query grounding head supervised against tumour segmentations
was trained under nested five-fold cross-validation, three seeds. The primary
endpoint was external generalisation of localisation, evaluated on 420 held-out
NSCLC-Radiomics patients (Maastro Clinic) against three controls: a per-fold
spatial shuffle, a centre prior, and a randomly-initialised head. A co-primary
endpoint — incremental AUC over a five-variable clinical model — was pre-declared
as an expected null. Analysis plan fixed and registered before external
evaluation.

**Results.** Pooled out-of-fold EGFR AUC was 0.618 ± 0.025, against 0.794 for
smoking status alone. Adding the model to clinical variables changed AUC by
−0.029 ± 0.015, every seed's CI including zero; no fusion topology (hard voting,
soft voting, cross-fitted stacking) exceeded the clinical model. Calibration slope
was 0.19 and the Brier score (0.236) exceeded the base-rate floor (0.193).
Localisation transferred: attention-mass lift +0.244 over the shuffle in 11 of 15
external evaluations, pointing 0.609 versus 0.476 for a centre prior and 0.005 for
a randomly-initialised head.

**Conclusion.** Supervised localisation generalises across institutions while
lesion-anchored EGFR prediction does not exceed a five-variable chart model.

## 1. Introduction (~350 words)

Three paragraphs, in this order.

1. **The clinical question and why imaging is attractive.** EGFR genotype directs
   TKI therapy; tissue is limited and testing is not universal. A non-invasive
   imaging surrogate would matter.
2. **What the literature reports, and the shape of the evidence.** AUC 0.80–0.89
   is standard (refs 6–11 in [REFERENCES.md](REFERENCES.md)); cohorts are 200–826,
   usually single-centre, external validation uncommon. The one large study with
   external validation — Rodríguez Sánchez et al., 1,646 patients and 11,473
   lesions — reports 0.62–0.68 internally and 0.55–0.63 externally. **That
   discrepancy is the paper's motivation** and should be stated in the
   introduction, not saved for the discussion.
3. **What this study adds.** Not a better classifier. A pre-specified test of two
   separable claims — that the model localises, and that it genotypes — with the
   controls each requires, on a cohort where the second claim can be measured
   against the clinical variables it would have to beat.

## 2. Materials and Methods (~600 words)

**2.1 Cohort.** NSCLC-Radiogenomics (TCIA), Stanford/VA Palo Alto. Prior reports:
Bakr 2018, Gevaert 2012/2017. Flow: 211 → 188 with imaging → 158 preprocessed →
153 with a known EGFR call → 133 adenocarcinoma → **97 segmented adenocarcinoma
(primary analysis set)**. *(Figure 1; Table 1.)*

No accrual window is reported: the collection's `CT Date` values span 1989–1996
and are de-identification offsets. Index-test-to-reference-standard interval:
median 38 days (IQR 19.5–65.5).

**2.2 External cohort.** NSCLC-Radiomics (Lung1, Maastro Clinic), 420 preprocessed
patients with segmentations. Zero patient overlap with the internal cohort; no
evaluated checkpoint was warm-started from it. Both asserted in the test suite.

**2.3 Model.** BiomedCLIP ViT-B/16, last two blocks unfrozen; region-query
grounding head (8 queries) supervised against the ROI on the patch grid; classifier
pooling from the grounded region embeddings. Crop `(bbox_mm + 24) × 2.5` with 0.30
jitter, 16 slices, HU window centre −600 width 1500. Full spec in
[MODEL_CARD.md](MODEL_CARD.md).

**2.4 Evaluation.** Nested five-fold CV stratified on EGFR, three seeds
(1337, 7, 42); within-fold rank normalisation before pooling. Headline is the
across-seed mean ± sd, never the pooled CI alone. **No operating point is defined**
— the index test is continuous and the calibration does not support a threshold.

**2.5 Endpoints and controls.** H₁ (primary): external attention-mass lift > 0,
against a per-fold spatial shuffle, a centre prior, and a randomly-initialised
head. H₂ (co-primary, pre-declared null): ΔAUC(clinical + model) − AUC(clinical).
Comparators: smoking status alone; five clinical variables; radiomics; four fusion
topologies.

**2.6 Statistics.** Paired bootstrap over patients (2000 resamples); Holm
correction across the ten pooling-sweep arms; Hanley–McNeil for precision; Riley
for minimum sample size, applied only where its inputs are defined.

## 3. Results (~700 words)

**3.1 The model does not beat the chart.** *(Table 2; Figures 2–3.)*

| arm | EGFR AUC |
|---|---|
| smoking status alone | **0.794** |
| clinical, five variables | 0.764 |
| soft voting (model + clinical) | 0.748 |
| cross-fitted stacking | 0.735 |
| **THEIA, 3 seeds** | **0.618 ± 0.025** |
| frozen-feature logistic probe | 0.617 ± 0.052 |
| radiomics | 0.526–0.662 |

ΔAUC over clinical = **−0.029 ± 0.015**, every seed's CI including zero. No fusion
topology exceeds the clinical model; adding radiomics significantly harms it
(−0.130, p = 0.020).

**3.2 It is also uncalibrated.** Slope 0.19 against a perfect 1.0. Brier 0.236
against a base-rate floor of 0.193 — the probabilities are worse than predicting
prevalence for every patient. Resolution 0.009–0.017.

**3.3 On the subset that should carry it, it is weaker still.** Every EGFR-mutant
is an adenocarcinoma and the 39 squamous/NSCLC-NOS patients are wild-type without
exception, so 13% of the cohort is classifiable from histology alone. On segmented
adenocarcinoma the AUC is **0.563**.

**3.4 Localisation generalises.** *(Figure 5.)* On 420 held-out patients: mass lift
**+0.244**, beating the shuffle in 11 of 15 evaluations; pointing 0.609 against
0.476 (centre prior) and 0.005 (random head).

**Two things must be said in the same breath.** The margin over the pre-specified
fold-rate threshold is **one fold** (11/15 = 73.3% against 71.4%). And the failures
are concentrated: `ms-s7` localises in 5/5, `ms-s42` in 4/5, and one run in **2/5**
— the same run that internally localises in 2/5 and has the *highest*
classification AUC.

**3.5 Analytic choice is worth more than the effect.** Radiomics moves **0.136
AUC** across six defensible preprocessing choices, and **0.066** between flat and
nested CV on identical features. Both exceed the effects this literature reports as
findings.

**3.6 Why more patients would not settle it.** Riley's minimum n is 414 for the
clinical model and 42,362 for the frozen probe; this cohort has 153. The entire
open world holds ~327 EGFR-labelled CT cases with ~64 positives, giving a 95% CI
half-width of ±0.154 — spanning chance and the published 0.80s at once.

## 4. Discussion (~350 words)

- The two claims are **separable and separate differently**. Localisation
  transfers; genotype prediction does not clear a five-variable chart model.
- Convergence with Rodríguez Sánchez et al. at 10× the patients argues the ceiling
  is a property of lesion-anchored CT, not of cohort size.
- The peritumoral signal replicates (six prior reports) but does not survive
  multiplicity correction here (p_holm 0.281).
- **Limitations, stated as limitations:** single internal cohort; no external
  validation of the classification arm, justified by the measured precision bound;
  no inter-rater ICC; one of three runs largely fails to localise; H₁ is
  confirmatory of a prior observation rather than a blind prospective test, and
  the timeline supporting that is in the registered plan.
