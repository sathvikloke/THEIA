## Preserve scientific distinctions

- Corrected M1 and historical M0 inputs/results remain separate. A current label named “Heart,” an IPP placement rule or a synthetic test does not establish historical clinical tumor truth.
- The retained overlap computation sampled 564 internal slices against 16,764 distinct nonconstant external digests and found zero matches. It does not establish person-level independence or exclude unsampled/transformed overlap.
- The historical cohort flow is 211 → 188 → 158 → 153; unknown selection reasons and approximate reconstructed exclusions stay explicit. `193 = 153 + 40` is not a demonstrated unique union.
- Logistic calibration-in-the-large is distinct from raw mean-risk minus prevalence. C06's 4/36 simulation qualification is post hoc null/binormal, not general clinical coverage.
- Three labelled TCGA starter cases and four CPTAC cases requiring label linkage are not seven already labelled external cases.
- Completed scripts/manifests/preparation and a clean subprocess exit are not accepted fits, human reads or independent new patients.
