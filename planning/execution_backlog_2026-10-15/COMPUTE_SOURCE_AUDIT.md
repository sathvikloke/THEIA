### Actual model, design and state

The **design** specifies six independent adaptations: **P1k/P3k/P10k × initialization seeds 42/7**. Every run begins from the original BiomedCLIP vision encoder (`24064fdbf02384327d1af5e3e1e7fb25106fd60b9f9812c03140a46077f7fbb3` under the project's tensor digest), never by continuing the smaller-scale run. The nested memberships/source/roles are frozen. The full executable training protocol is **still a draft**: `P6/ADAPTATION_RUNNER_INPUTS.json` records 21 unresolved/draft values, including fixed update budget U, augmentation/view scale, slice selection, exact negatives semantics, schedule and device/precision. A claim that every training setting is already frozen would be false.

The implemented prototype is a 2-D BiomedCLIP ViT-B/16 (224², native 768-dimensional patch tokens), updating the final two blocks and final norm, **26 tensors / 14,177,280 parameters**, plus a disposable **768→768→GELU→128** projector. It averages native patch tokens for the contrastive objective, uses L2-normalized embeddings and symmetric NT-Xent with prototype temperature 0.2. Each batch is distinct people, one selected slice/person, two views of that same slice; other people provide negatives. The proposed batch32 has 62 negatives/anchor. Ordinary gradient accumulation cannot turn small simultaneous batches into this objective.

Actual source exists in `PCT/code/adaptation/{model.py,ntxent.py,views.py,train_step.py,transfer.py,bench_synthetic.py}`. `train_step.py` implements AdamW over the allowed set, gradient clipping, nonfinite-step rejection, warmup/cosine helper and an exposure log. `BW/public_ct/scripts/exposure_sampler.py` implements distinct-person batches and applied-update exposure accounting. **There is no scientific adaptation training runner on this inspected path.** `P6/adaptation_runner_skeleton.py` explicitly has no training code and no success exit: 2 for incomplete inputs, 3 even when inputs are complete. It is not an executable training job.

The real-encoder synthetic benchmark ran 200 steps in October 3 evidence (`PCT/MICROBENCH_MEASURED.json`): six CPU threads, median 1.814118 s/step, peak RSS 3,183,181,824 bytes, 14,866,304 trainable parameters including the temporary projector, 32 synthetic examples / 64 views / 62 negatives per anchor. It is tensor-path engineering evidence, not NLST training, derivative throughput or an unfinished benchmark allowance. Latest source/closure evidence establishes **no completed scientific adaptation, no accepted downstream adapted fit and no real held-out reconstruction result**. October 14's 2,391 verified archives (all P1k, partial P3k/P10k) do not change those computational counters. The inspected live tree also has no `theia/models/adapted_transfer.py`.

The downstream matrix is **30 adapted cell-B fits** = three scales × two seeds × five fixed partition1337 folds, plus **10 matched baseline fits only if N08 B cannot be reused**. Reuse requires actual patient/split/transform/optimizer/selection/software and downstream projection/head/classifier starting-weight compatibility. The primary comparison is P10k adapted minus matched baseline **patient-level EGFR log loss**, averaged over the two seed-specific contrasts; negative favours adaptation. P1k/P3k, AUC/Brier/calibration/spatial quantities are secondary/exploratory. No smaller achieved scale can silently replace P10k as primary.

### Exact interfaces and implementation gaps

1. **Skeleton only:** verified parser accepts `--inputs`, `--root`, `--no-verify-sources`. For example, `.venv/bin/python "$P6/adaptation_runner_skeleton.py" --inputs "$P6/ADAPTATION_RUNNER_INPUTS.json" --root "$PROJECT_ROOT"` checks the incomplete contract and refuses; it does not train even if inputs become complete. Do not publish `run_adaptation.py --scale ...` as a working command: `P10_READY_COMMANDS.md` explicitly labels that a future placeholder.
2. **Training primitives:** `AdaptationModule.pooled/forward`, `make_optimizer`, `warmup_cosine`, `train_step`, `ExposureLog` and `ExposureSampler` are actual functions. `train_step` currently calls the fixed NT-Xent module constant; a future configurable protocol requires explicit plumbing. Different series of one patient cannot enter as negatives under different keys.
3. **Transfer candidate:** `model.adapted_vision_payload` is a **proposed** `--set` key from the unmerged trainer patch, not a valid working feature of current live training. Use normal N08 B argv only after integrating/testing that hook and explicit M1 data bindings. `P6/ADAPTATION_RUNNER_INPUTS.json` warns that candidate metadata beyond start/format/allowlist is carried but not all validated.
4. **Primary statistics:** `primary_logloss.py` has `analyse(oof, labels, fold, B=10000, seed=20261003)`, no argparse CLI. It jointly excludes unknown/missing/nonfinite patient entries; current caller must explicitly verify a nonempty, intended eligible cohort and report actual exclusions.
