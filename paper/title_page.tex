\noindent\textbf{Summary statement.}
Weakly supervised tumour localisation transferred from a United States surgical
cohort to a Dutch radiotherapy cohort, whereas prediction of epidermal growth
factor receptor mutation status from the same lesion-anchored computed
tomographic images did not exceed a five-variable model built from routinely
recorded clinical data.
